## S3 — Recommended Formats Statement: Audio Works (Library of Congress)

- URL: https://www.loc.gov/preservation/resources/rfs/audio.html
- Title: Recommended Formats Statement — Audio Works
- Publication date: 2025–2026 edition
- Tier: 1 — national library / primary preservation guidance
- Evidence path: `research/sources/gap-governance-02-loc-audio-formats.md`

> “WAVE file with embedded metadata (Broadcast WAVE) rather than without embedded metadata.”

> “File in native resolution rather than up-sampled resolution” and “Uncompressed files rather than compressed.”

> Preferred high-resolution audio is “96kHz/24bit or higher PCM”; acceptable audio includes “44.1 kHz/16 bit” PCM WAVE, with lossless preferred over lossy compression.

> Metadata may include title, artist, composer, genre, publisher, location and date of performance, publication date, and standard or entity identifiers.

## S4 — ActivityStreams 2.0 Vocabulary (W3C)

- URL: https://www.w3.org/TR/activitystreams-vocabulary
- Title: Activity Vocabulary
- Publication date: 23 May 2017
- Tier: 1 — W3C Recommendation / normative standard
- Evidence path: `research/sources/gap-governance-03-activitystreams-vocabulary.md`

> “This specification describes the Activity vocabulary. It is intended to be used in the context of the ActivityStreams 2.0 format and provides a foundational vocabulary for activity structures, and specific activity types.”

> Objects can carry `attributedTo`, `audience`, `context`, `generator`, `inReplyTo`, `location`, `published`, `tag`, `updated`, and addressing properties; Activities add `actor`, `object`, `target`, `result`, `origin`, and `instrument`.

> A `Tombstone` can retain `formerType`, while `deleted` records “a timestamp for when the object was deleted.”

## S6 — Guidelines for Social Justice Oral History Work (OHA)

- URL: https://oralhistory.org/guidelines-for-social-justice-oral-history-work/
- Title: Guidelines for Social Justice Oral History Work
- Publication date: adopted 2022
- Tier: 2 — professional association guidance
- Evidence path: `research/sources/gap-governance-snippets.md`

> “Co-determined interview usage (rolling consent requires reconfirmation for interviews utilized outside mutual understanding).”

> The guidance calls for co-created access levels centered first on narrators, their families and associates, and community, and asks that key documents be translated into the participant’s language.

## S8 — Oral-history ethics and withdrawal (COHDS)

- URL: https://storytelling.concordia.ca/resources/ethics
- Title: Ethics
- Publication date: n.d. (accessed 8 September 2026)
- Tier: 2 — university oral-history research centre guidance
- Evidence path: `research/sources/gap-governance-snippets.md`

> Ethical review centers “informed consent, the mitigation of harm and the right of withdrawal.”

> Interviewees need to know the project’s purpose, intended uses of the audio-video recording and words, and whether future researchers will have access.
